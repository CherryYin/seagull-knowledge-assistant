# Review Discovery Batch

## Workflow
1. Inspect all supplied discovery candidates.
2. Cluster them by reusable topic rather than by source site.
3. Rank the strongest discoveries by relevance, novelty, and evidence quality.
4. Identify which items deserve saving and which topics need deeper research.
5. Flag topics with credible wiki potential.
6. Do not save or mutate anything automatically.

## Required Output
## Top Discoveries
## Topic Clusters
## What To Save
## What To Research Next
## Wiki Potential

Allowed future save targets are note, review, and memory candidate after explicit confirmation.
