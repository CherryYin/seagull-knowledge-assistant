# Mine Wiki Candidates

## Workflow
1. Search the available knowledge context before proposing candidates.
2. Cluster recurring concepts and discard one-off noisy items.
3. Require multiple supporting pieces of evidence where possible.
4. Check whether a stable wiki already exists for each candidate.
5. Recommend either refreshing an existing wiki or starting a reviewable new draft.
6. Keep every recommendation reversible and do not save automatically.

## Required Output
## Candidate Topics
## Why These Matter
## Supporting Evidence
## Open Gaps
## Review Queue Recommendation

Reference evidence IDs. Allowed future save targets are review, wiki refresh, and memory candidate after explicit confirmation.
