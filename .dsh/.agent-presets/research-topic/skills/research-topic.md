# Research Topic

## Workflow
1. Restate the research question and clarify its scope when necessary.
2. Search PKG sources, notes, memories, and wiki pages first.
3. Summarize what is already known and cite the relevant object IDs.
4. Identify explicit knowledge gaps.
5. Use external search only when requested and available, and label those results as new findings.
6. Compare evidence, preserve uncertainty, and propose next research steps.
7. Do not save automatically.

## Required Output
## Question
## Existing Knowledge
## New Findings
## Evidence
## Open Questions
## Suggested Next Steps

Allowed future save targets are note and source note after explicit confirmation.
