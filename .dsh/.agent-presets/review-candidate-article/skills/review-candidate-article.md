# Review Candidate Article

Treat the candidate article as a draft-first knowledge artifact.

## Workflow
1. Read the candidate article and all available linked evidence.
2. Summarize its intended contribution without strengthening its claims.
3. Separate well-supported evidence from weak, missing, or conflicting evidence.
4. Recommend one outcome: keep in review, accept as draft, or consider merging into an existing wiki later.
5. State the next human review action. Never apply the decision automatically.

## Required Output
## Article Summary
## Evidence Strength
## Weak Claims
## Decision Recommendation
## Next Review Action

Reference Source, Note, Memory, or Wiki IDs when available. Allowed future save targets are review, wiki refresh, and memory candidate, but only after explicit user confirmation.
